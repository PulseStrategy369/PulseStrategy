● Immutable — no admin keys 10,000 PLS + min 10 to launch 3.5% stays in the vault 0.5% protocol · 0.5% creator Redemption never gated

Create a vault Name it. Pick a backing asset. Pick 5% or 10%. Pay 10,000 PLS and mint at least 10 of the asset in the same transaction. Then the contract is yours, ownerless, on PulseChain.

Each 7-day epoch only that percent of current supply can be minted. 5% on a supply of 100 means 5 new tokens this week; 10% means 10. After the cap fills, buy on PulseX until the next epoch. The week you launch is uncapped — same as PLSstr — so the vault can bootstrap. From the next epoch the 5% or 10% cap applies, holds 13 weeks, then decays to 1% over 39 weeks, then stays at 1%.

PLSX

You receive vault tokens at backing +4.5%. Min 10 of the chosen asset, in the same transaction as the 10,000 PLS fee. That puts the token in your wallet. The rest of this 7-day epoch stays uncapped, like PLSstr.

Launch costs 10,000 PLS plus at least 10 of the backing asset (and a little gas). Empty vaults cannot be created. Immutable — no switch, no waiver.

How it works Three moving parts. No graduation.

Mint — the ceiling

Deposit the backing asset, receive vault tokens at backing +4.5%. 3.5% stays in the vault. 0.5% protocol fee. 0.5% to the creator. Minting is capped per 7-day epoch; when the quota fills, the DEX is the only source until reset.

Hold — the ratchet

Backing per token can only rise. Premiums, the redeem fee, and donations feed it. There is no admin key and no pause switch.

Redeem — the floor

Burn vault tokens any time for your pro-rata share minus 0.5% that stays behind. Never gated. Last redeemer pays nothing so the vault cannot trap dust.

Not a pump launch. Nothing here seeds a pool, locks LP, or "graduates." You get a vault contract, and you already hold its token from the required first mint (min 10 of the backing asset) plus a 10,000 PLS create fee. Empty vaults cannot be created. Want a market? Open the vault tab and create a PulseX v2 pool from this file. The board ranks vault TVL plus whatever PulseX depth it can see.

What is guaranteed: backing-per-token in the vault's asset never decreases from any operation of these contracts. What is not: market price, the price of the backing asset, or anything below that asset.

Leaderboard Rank = vault TVL + PulseX depth. Switch PLS / USD for price, cap, TVL, depth, and volume. Floor is backing-per-token. 24h / 7d from chain logs — no indexer.

      Using a downloaded copy

      Everything here works as soon as you open it. Connecting a wallet to sign a mint, redeem, or launch takes one extra step, because browsers keep downloaded files separate from websites.


      On a computer — let your wallet see local files: open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions), find your wallet, click Details, and turn on Allow access to file URLs. Then connect with Browser wallet.


      On Android — open the file using Cx File Explorer, a free app on the official Google Play store. In its Open-with list, choose Chrome or Brave — the ones marked HTTP. WalletConnect then works normally.


      On iPhone or iPad — a downloaded copy is fine for reading, but iOS gives no equivalent way to serve it, so use pulsestrategy369.com/launch to connect a wallet.
