THE FLOOR ONLY RISES.

PulseVerify is free, open, and serverless — built and given away by PulseStrategy, a family of ownerless vault tokens designed around one rule: the amount of backing behind each token can never go down. Not "shouldn't." Cannot.
  • No owner. No admin keys. No pause switch, no upgrades, no governance — the rules can never change.
  • The floor ratchets up. Every mint adds more backing than tokens; every redemption leaves value behind.
  • 100% publicly minted. Zero team allocation, zero pre-mine, zero emissions.
  • Holders collect the churn. Every round trip through a vault leaves ~4.3% behind for people who simply hold.

Using a downloaded copy
Everything here works as soon as you open it — blocks, prices, verification and safety scans need no wallet at all. Connecting a wallet is only for signing, and takes one extra step because browsers keep downloaded files separate from websites.

On a computer — let your wallet see local files: open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions), find your wallet, click Details, and turn on Allow access to file URLs. Then connect with Browser wallet.

On Android — open the file using Cx File Explorer, a free app on the official Google Play store (get it here). In its Open-with list, choose Chrome or Brave — the ones marked HTTP. WalletConnect then works normally.

On iPhone or iPad — a downloaded copy is fine for reading, but iOS gives no equivalent way to serve it, so use pulsestrategy369.com to connect a wallet.
